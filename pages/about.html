Focusly started as a side project between two developers tired of task managers that were either too simple or overwhelmingly complex. Today it's used by 50,000 people every day.

Our Story

In 2022, Bohdan and Maria built the first version of Focusly for themselves. They shared it with a few friends. Those friends shared it with their teams. Within a year, 10,000 people were using it without a single ad.

We've kept the team small on purpose. Eight people across four countries, all remote, all using Focusly every single day to manage our own work.

2022
Founded as a side project. First 100 users.

Reached 10,000 users. Launched mobile app for iOS and Android.
2024

2025
AI Suggestions shipped. 50,000 users across 80 countries.

What We Stand For

Simplicity first

Every feature proposal starts with one question: does this help people focus or distract them? If it distracts, it doesn't ship.

Respect your time

Setup takes minutes, not hours. There are no onboarding videos, no 12-step tutorials, no mandatory tours.

Privacy by default

We don't run ads. We don't sell data. We don't analyze your content. Your tasks are yours.
